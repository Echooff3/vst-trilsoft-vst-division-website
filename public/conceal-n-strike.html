Briefing

TWO HANDS. EVERY STEP.

A scratch DJ does two jobs at once. Conceal-N-Strike splits them up and lets you program each one, step by step.

LEFT HAND // THE PLATTER

CONCEAL

How the record moves under the needle. Baby scratches, tears, scribbles, drags, spinbacks and more — each with its own knob for throw, tension, curve or speed.

RIGHT HAND // THE CROSSFADER

STRIKE

How the sound gets cut. Stabs, chirps, transformers, flares, crabs and fades — with a real fader throw, so sharp curves cut instantly and smooth ones fade.

AB

CONCEAL + STRIKE × 64 steps = a full routine, played back in time

BPM

Locked to the beat

Follows your DAW's tempo and playhead, or runs its own clock. Step resolution from 1/4 to 1/32 (triplets and dotted too), plus swing.

WAV

Load your own ammo

Drop in a WAV, MP3, AIFF, FLAC, OGG or M4A — or scratch the built-in “ahh”. Click the waveform to set the cue.

8×8

8 sector banks

Eight pages of eight steps. Follow the playhead, set the pattern length anywhere from 1 to 64, and copy/paste whole pages.

CUE

CUE or RIDE

CUE lands every step on the same sound. RIDE lets the record carry on from wherever the last move left it.

DAW

Automate everything

Every step's four controls are host parameters, so your DAW can automate them, undo edits and save them with the project.

DIV

The Wild Card engine

Built on the same scratch engine as Wild Card: band-limited playback at any speed and a record that follows the hand.

The arsenal

Pick an action for each hand on every step, then dial its knob in.
