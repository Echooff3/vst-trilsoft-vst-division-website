DIV-002 // Field manual

CONCEAL-N-STRIKE

Weird VSTs no one asked for.

A 64-step turntablism sequencer. Every step programs both of a DJ's hands: how the record moves, and how the fader cuts it. Scroll for the full briefing.

01 // Setup

Run the installer

One macOS installer deploys every format: VST3, CLAP, an Audio Unit for Logic Pro and GarageBand, and a standalone app.

Only want some? Hit Customize on the Installation Type step and skip the rest.

02 // Setup

Drop it on an instrument track

Conceal-N-Strike loads like a synth. It doesn't play notes from MIDI; it just needs a track to live on. Hit play in your DAW and it runs.

No DAW? The standalone app runs on its own clock.

03 // Setup

Activate once

On first launch it asks for your activation code. It's in your purchase email and on the thank-you page right after checkout. Paste it, hit Activate. After that it checks your license offline.

  • PASTE for hosts that swallow ⌘V
  • CONC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX
04 // Orientation

Know the panel

  • COMMAND CLOCK tempo, sync, step timing, swing, curve
  • PAYLOAD the sample you're cutting
  • SECTOR BANKS eight pages of eight steps
  • CHANNEL STRIPS the eight steps on the page you're looking at

Out of the box it's loaded with a built-in sample and a demo routine, so press play first and listen.

05 // Command clock

Sync: DAW or INT

DAW follows your host's tempo and playhead, and the pattern lines up with your bars. The light reads HOST ROLL while the host plays.

INT runs its own clock at the MASTER BPM: hit PLAY/STOP and drag the readout to set the tempo, 20 to 300 BPM.

Standalone app: always INT, and Space plays or stops.

06 // Command clock

Step res & swing

STEP RES sets how long each step is: 1/4 down to 1/32, plus triplets (1/8T, 1/16T) and dotted (1/8D, 1/16D).

SWING runs from 50% to 75% and pushes every second step late.

07 // Payload

Load your sample

It starts on the BUILT-IN "ahh", the classic scratch vowel. Hit LOAD for your own WAV, MP3, AIFF, FLAC, OGG or M4A. BUILT-IN takes you back.

OUTPUT sets the level.

08 // Payload

Set the cue

Click the waveform to set the CUE point, the spot every move works from. Load a new sample and its cue goes where its sound starts.

The 16 numbered slices are what DICE jumps between.

09 // Payload

Sample edge

What the record finds past the ends of your sample:

  • LOOP back in at the start (the default)
  • BOUNCE plays back the way it came
  • SILENT dead air
10 // Channel strip

One step, two hands

Each column is one step. The top half is CONCEAL, the platter: how the record moves. The bottom half is STRIKE, the crossfader: how the sound gets cut.

The red light marks the step that's playing.

11 // Channel strip

Pick an action

  • CLICK PAD open the chooser with every action
  • RIGHT-CLICK cycle to the next action
  • ESC close the chooser

Picking an action sets its knob to that action's default, so it sounds right straight away.

12 // Conceal arsenal 1 of 2

Platter moves

  • FORWARD push it. Knob: speed 0.25×–2×
  • REVERSE pull it. Knob: speed 0.25×–2×
  • BABY the classic back and forth. Knob: throw, 10–150% of a step
  • TEAR a pull-back broken by a pause. Knob: split
  • SCRIBBLE a tense, shaking hand. Knob: tension, 6 Hz shake to 60 Hz buzz
13 // Conceal arsenal 2 of 2

More moves

  • DRAG slows the record. Knob: gentle slow-down to sudden brake
  • DICE jumps to one of 16 slices, then plays on. Knob: slice
  • SPIN/BRAKE left half brakes, right half spins back at 2×–10×
  • HYDRO hydroplane. Knob: friction, the slip-and-grip rate and depth
14 // Strike arsenal 1 of 2

Fader cuts

  • OPEN fader up. Knob: level
  • MUTE fader shut. Knob: bleed, 0–20%
  • STAB a short burst. Knob: gate, 5–60% of the step
  • CHIRP cut out the middle. Knob: pinch, how wide the gap is
15 // Strike arsenal 2 of 2

More cuts

  • TRANSFORM 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8 cuts per step
  • FLARE 1, 2 or 3 clicks
  • CRAB a four-finger roll. Knob: roll speed, over the whole step or the first quarter
  • FADE left of center fades out, right of center swells in
16 // Channel strip

Dial it in

Every pad has a knob. The readout underneath tells you exactly what it's set to: THROW 80%, 4 CUTS, SPIN 6.0X.

  • DRAG up or down
  • SHIFT fine steps
  • DOUBLE-CLICK back to the default
17 // Command clock

XF curve

XF CURVE shapes every STRIKE. The fader cap has a real 8 ms throw, and the hand only pushes it just past the curve's cut-in point.

So a sharp curve makes near-instant cuts, and a smooth one gives short fades.

18 // Command clock

Anchor: CUE or RIDE

CUE (the default): every move is placed so it never goes behind the cue point. Forward moves start at the cue, pulls end there, so every step cuts the same sound.

RIDE: the record carries on from wherever the last move left it, and goes back to the cue at the top of each loop.

19 // Sector banks

64 steps, 8 pages

Click a bank to see its eight steps. FOLLOW turns the page as the playhead moves on, and you can still peek at another page until it does.

LEN sets the pattern length, 1 to 64: use the arrows or drag the readout. Steps past it go dim and read OFFLINE.

20 // Sector banks

Copy & paste

COPY grabs every step on the page you're on. Go to another bank and hit PASTE to drop it there, then tweak the differences.

21 // Field notes

Pro tips

  • AUTOMATE every step's four controls are DAW parameters: automate them, undo them, save them with the project
  • RECORD what comes out is the scratched sample, locked to tempo. Bounce or record it like any instrument
  • SPACE plays and stops in the standalone app
