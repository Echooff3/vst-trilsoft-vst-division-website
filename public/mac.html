SCROLL

Header

Count him in

The play button starts him, or stops him at the next bar. Status reads STOPPED, COUNTING IN, PLAYING, or LAST HIT!

START WITH HOST starts him with the host transport. It begins off. SAME TAKE plays the same performance every time. NEW TAKE rolls new dice on each start. Same Take is the start. REROLL picks new dice for the Same Take, and the project keeps them.

Standalone: Space starts and stops. F is a fill. 1 to 8 are the sections.

Mac only

The Rage paw

How hard he gets pushed. Low is cute: ghost notes, grooves as written. High is louder, more crashes, then a d-beat, then a blast. His face follows the knob. It runs 0 to 100%, and starts at 35%.

Scroll on this page is the same idea: cute, then grumpy, then rage.

His mood

Calm to furious

The pad is CALM to FURIOUS across, and SIMPLE to FANCY up. The readout is ANGER and FANCY. The puck picks grooves inside the Variation circle. Drag to move. Double-click resets. It starts at 50% across and 30% up. In Beatdown the pad shows breakdown patterns instead.

Sections

Eight calls

INTRO, VERSE, CHORUS, D-BEAT, TWO-STEP, BREAKDOWN, HARDCORE, PUNK. PHRASE is the bar in the phrase, orange during a fill. FILL! plays a fill now.

How hard

Fills and variation

FILLS is how often. 0 is none. Halfway is the end of each phrase. The top is FILL MANIA! It starts at 35%.

VARIATION at 0 locks to the groove under the puck. Higher widens the pick circle and adds ghost notes, extra kicks, and looser timing. It starts at 7%.

The take

Drag it out

The film strip records everything he plays. DRAG ME drops it in the DAW as a MIDI clip. CLEAR empties the strip.

Beatdown

Chugs on the sidechain

BEATDOWN latches breakdown mode. Sidechain chugs trigger a kick plus a crash, and the crashes alternate. C0 does it, and so does B in the standalone app. It starts off.

THRESHOLD is how big a rise counts as a chug. Lower catches softer palm mutes. It starts at 30%. HITS is SINGLE, TRIPLETS from 28%, or QUAD BLAST from 72%, locked to the tempo. Single is the start.

CHUGS is sidechain hits only. GROOVE is a half-time bed the puck picks. BOTH is the two together, and it's the start. In Chugs, SNARE is how often a chug on beat 1 or 3 also hits the snare. It starts at 35%. Otherwise the line reads PUCK PICKS THE BED.

The sidechain meter runs from -60 to 0 dB and goes red above -12. TRIG flashes on each chug. The stereo input is named Sidechain. The pad patterns are HALF, AND, DOT, PUSH, WASH, BELL, CHINA, STEP, CALL, and MOSH.

Tempo

Host, or the box

HOST follows the DAW. SET is the BPM box, 40 to 300. Shift-drag for fine steps. The box is hidden in the standalone app. Host is the start, and the box starts at 160. HALF, NORMAL, and DOUBLE are the feel. Normal is the start.

Phrasing

Bars, then when

BARS is 4, 8, or 16. Fills land at the ends. 8 is the start. START is NOW, the next BEAT, or the next BAR. Bar is the start. Section changes wait for the same choice.

Keyswitches

From the keys

FROM is the lowest keyswitch. It starts at C-1. Order: start or stop, fill, then the eight sections. CHANNEL is ANY or 1 to 16. Any is the start. Last heard shows the last one. C0 toggles Beatdown and wins over that bank.

Output

Where the notes go

CH is the drum channel. It starts at 10. Velocity runs 0 to 200% and starts at 100%. MAP remaps each drum's note. GENERAL MIDI puts it back.

The port menu sends MIDI somewhere else as well as the host: HOST ONLY, VIRTUAL PORT "Mac", or a device. Host only is the start.

Kit

A kit to hear him

OFF or DRUMS. Off in a DAW. Drums in the standalone app. The slider is the kit volume, -36 to +6 dB, starting at -3.
