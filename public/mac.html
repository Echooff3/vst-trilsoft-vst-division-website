01 · HEADER

Count him in

The play button starts him, or stops him at the next bar. Status reads STOPPED, COUNTING IN, PLAYING, or LAST HIT!

START WITH HOST starts with the host transport. It begins off. SAME TAKE plays the same performance every time. NEW TAKE rolls new dice on each start. REROLL picks new dice for the Same Take, and the project keeps them.

Standalone: Space starts and stops. Play, Stop, and Fill are also one-shot triggers for Max for Live and MIDI mapping.

02 · RAGE

The Rage paw

Mac only. How hard he gets pushed. Low is cute: ghost notes, grooves as written. High is louder, more crashes, then a d-beat, then a blast. His face follows the knob.

0 to 100%. It starts at 35%. Scroll the top of this page and the card does the same: cute, grumpy, rage.

03 · HIS MOOD

Calm to furious

The pad is CALM to FURIOUS across, and SIMPLE to FANCY up. The readout is ANGER and FANCY. The puck picks grooves inside the Variation circle. Drag to move. Double-click resets.

It starts at 50% across and 30% up. In Beatdown the pad shows breakdown patterns instead.

04 · SECTIONS

Eight calls

INTRO, VERSE, CHORUS, D-BEAT, TWO-STEP, BREAKDOWN, HARDCORE, PUNK. PHRASE is the bar in the phrase, and it goes orange during a fill. FILL! plays a fill now.

Standalone: keys 1 to 8.

05 · HOW HARD

Fills, up to mania

FILLS is how often. 0 is none. Halfway is the end of each phrase. The top is FILL MANIA! It starts at 35%.

FILL! asks for one right now. In the standalone app, so does F.

06 · VARIATION

The pick circle

VARIATION at 0 locks to the groove under the puck. Higher widens the pick circle and adds ghost notes, extra kicks, and looser timing. It starts at 7%.

07 · THE TAKE

Drag the take

The film strip records everything he plays. DRAG ME drops it in the DAW as a MIDI clip. CLEAR empties the strip.

08 · BEATDOWN

Chugs on the sidechain

BEATDOWN latches breakdown mode. Sidechain chugs trigger a kick plus a crash, and the crashes alternate. C0 does it, and so does B in the standalone app. It starts off.

THRESHOLD starts at 30%. Lower catches softer palm mutes. HITS is Single, Triplets from 28%, or Quad Blast from 72%. CHUGS, GROOVE, or BOTH. Both is the start. In Chugs, SNARE starts at 35%. Otherwise the line reads PUCK PICKS THE BED.

The meter runs from -60 to 0 dB and goes red above -12. TRIG flashes on each chug. Patterns: HALF, AND, DOT, PUSH, WASH, BELL, CHINA, STEP, CALL, MOSH.

09 · TEMPO

Host, or the box

HOST follows the DAW. SET is the BPM box, 40 to 300. Shift-drag for fine steps. The box is hidden in the standalone app. Host is the start, and the box starts at 160.

HALF, NORMAL, and DOUBLE are the feel. Normal is the start.

10 · PHRASING

Bars, then when

BARS is 4, 8, or 16. Fills land at the ends. 8 is the start. START is NOW, the next BEAT, or the next BAR. Bar is the start. Section changes wait for the same choice.

11 · KEYSWITCHES

From the keys

  • C-1Start, or stop
  • C#-1A fill, now
  • D-1 – A-1Sections 1 to 8
  • C0Beatdown. It wins over the bank.

FROM is the lowest keyswitch. It starts at C-1. CHANNEL is ANY or 1 to 16. Last heard shows the last one.

12 · OUTPUT

Where the notes go

CH starts at 10. Velocity runs 0 to 200% and starts at 100%. MAP remaps each drum. GENERAL MIDI puts it back.

The port menu sends MIDI somewhere else as well as the host: HOST ONLY, VIRTUAL PORT "Mac", or a device. Host only is the start.

13 · KIT

Preview kit

OFF or DRUMS. Off in a DAW, where your drum plug-in is the sound. Drums in the standalone app. The slider is the preview volume, from -36 to +6 dB, starting at -3.

What those preview drums are is not decided yet. This is the control, not a sample credit.
