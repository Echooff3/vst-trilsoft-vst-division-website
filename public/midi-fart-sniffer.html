01 · SETUP

Run the installer

One macOS installer drops every format: VST3, CLAP, an Audio Unit, the standalone app, and the Max for Live device.

Skip what you don't play. Customize the Installation Type step and leave the rest in the can.

02 · SETUP

It feeds your drums

MIDI Fart Sniffer doesn't make the drum sound. It plays MIDI into the drum plug-in you already like: EZdrummer, Battery, a Drum Rack, anything. Put it where that MIDI can reach the instrument.

No host? The standalone app sniffs on its own, with its preview kit, and can send its MIDI out a port.

03 · SETUP

Activate once

It's free. Get a key above, and the first launch asks for it. Paste it in and the license stays on the machine.

04 · DUMPSTER

Open a folder

DUMPSTER is the folder on show. ▲ goes up a folder and OPEN picks another. Type in the box to sniff for a name. FAVES holds every groove you starred, from any folder.

05 · SNIFF

Click it. It plays.

Click a groove and, with AUTO on, it plays. Click it again to stop. The next one carries on from the same beat of the bar, so a whole folder stays in time. Stop the DAW and it stops too.

Standalone app: Up and Down sniff the next one.

06 · THE CARD

Every groove gets a card

Lanes are the drums, with General MIDI names. Hits puff as the playhead goes by. Click a lane's name to mute that drum. Click the lanes or the slime tube to jump. The star faves it.

07 · TEMPO

HOST, FILE, or SET

HOST follows the DAW. FILE uses the groove's own tempo, or a "126BPM" in its name. SET is the box: drag it. TIME plays it half or double.

LAUNCH waits for the DAW's next beat or bar, then stays locked to it. NOW starts at once and runs free.

08 · OUTPUT

Where the MIDI goes

CHANNEL puts every note on one channel, or FILE leaves them where the groove put them. VELOCITY scales them, 0 to 200%.

ALSO SEND TO copies the MIDI to a port: an IAC bus, a drum machine, or VIRTUAL PORT, which other apps see as an input named MIDI Fart Sniffer.

09 · DRAG

Drag it into the DAW

Grab the banner that says DRAG ME, or any groove in the list, and drop it on a track in Live, GarageBand, or Logic. It lands as a MIDI clip.

The file, not the edit. Lane mutes, channel, and velocity stay in the plug-in.

10 · PREVIEW

Drums, or farts

PREVIEW KIT is optional and starts OFF in a DAW, because your drum plug-in is the sound. DRUMS is a small kit for a quick listen. FARTS is exactly what it says.

11 · ABLETON

Right in front of the drums

Live won't put a third-party plug-in in the MIDI-effect slot, so the installer brings a Max for Live device. Drop it before your drums. OPEN THE DUMPSTER shows the window; RIP IT and HOLD IT start and stop it without opening it again.

No Max for Live? Sniffer on one track, drums on another: set the drums' MIDI From to the Sniffer track, pick MIDI Fart Sniffer under it, monitor In.

12 · LOGIC

Use the MIDI FX slot

The Audio Unit shows up as an instrument and as a MIDI processor. Put it in the MIDI FX slot of a drum track and it plays that track's instrument.

You can still play. Notes you send in pass straight through.
