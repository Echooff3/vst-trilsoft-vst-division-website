01 · Setup

Run the installer

One macOS installer drops every format: VST3, CLAP, an Audio Unit, the standalone app, and the Max for Live device.

Skip what you don't play. Customize the Installation Type step and leave the rest in the can.

02 · Setup

It feeds your drums

MIDI Fart Sniffer does not replace EZdrummer, Battery, or a Drum Rack. It plays MIDI into them. Put it where that MIDI can reach the instrument.

No host clock? The standalone app still sniffs. HOST tempo reads the file, and you send MIDI out a port.

04 · Dumpster

Open a folder

DUMPSTER is the folder on show. ▲ goes up. OPEN picks another. Type in the box to filter. FAVES is every groove you starred, from any folder, shared by every instance.

05 · Sniff

Click it. Arrow down.

Click a groove and, with AUTO on, it plays. Click it again to stop. The next groove picks up from the same beat of the bar, so a folder stays in time.

Standalone app: Down loads the next one. Inside a host, click. The search box has to own the keyboard in there.

06 · The card

Every groove gets a card

Lanes are the drums, General MIDI names when it knows them. Hits puff as the playhead goes by. Click a lane name to mute that drum. Click the slime tube to jump. The star faves it.

07 · Tempo

HOST, FILE, or SET

HOST follows the DAW. FILE uses the tempo in the MIDI file, or a "126BPM" in the name. SET is the box. Drag it. TIME is half or double.

LAUNCH waits for the next beat or bar, then stays locked to the host. NOW starts immediately and runs free.

08 · Output

Where the MIDI goes

CHANNEL puts every note on one channel, or FILE leaves them where the groove put them. VELOCITY scales them, 0 to 200%.

ALSO SEND TO copies that MIDI to a port: an IAC bus, a drum machine, or VIRTUAL PORT. Other apps see an input named MIDI Fart Sniffer. That's how the standalone app records into a DAW.

09 · Drag

Drag the card into the DAW

On a Mac, drag the banner that says DRAG ME, or any groove in the list, onto a track in Live, GarageBand, or Logic. It drops the original MIDI file.

The file, not the edit. Lane mutes, channel, and velocity stay in the plug-in. They don't bake into the clip.

10 · Preview

Drums, or farts

PREVIEW KIT is optional. OFF in a host, because your drum plug-in is the sound. DRUMS is a small kit for a quick listen. FARTS is the other one. The standalone app opens on DRUMS.

11 · Ableton

Two ways into Live

Live won't put a third-party plug-in in the MIDI-effect slot. The slick way needs Suite or Max for Live: drop MIDI Fart Sniffer.amxd in front of the drums and click OPEN THE DUMPSTER. Your own playing still passes through.

Or two tracks. Sniffer on one, drums on the other. Set the drums' MIDI From to the Sniffer track, then pick MIDI Fart Sniffer under that, and monitor In.

12 · Logic

Use the MIDI FX slot

The Audio Unit shows up as an instrument and as a MIDI processor. Put it in the MIDI FX slot of a drum track and it plays that track's instrument.

You can still play. Notes you send in pass straight through to the output.
