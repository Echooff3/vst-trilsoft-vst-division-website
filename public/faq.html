01 MIDI Fart Sniffer

Is MIDI Fart Sniffer free?

Yes. The price is Free. On the MIDI Fart Sniffer page, enter an email and hit Get Free Key. The key is sent to that address. The email is only used to send the key and is stored hashed. See Privacy.

How does MIDI Fart Sniffer get into Ableton Live?

Live won't put a third-party plug-in in the MIDI-effect slot. With Suite or Max for Live, drop MIDI Fart Sniffer.amxd in front of the drums and click OPEN THE DUMPSTER. Your own playing still passes through. Or use two tracks. Put the sniffer on one and the drums on the other. Set the drums' MIDI From to the sniffer track, pick MIDI Fart Sniffer under that, and monitor In.

Where does MIDI Fart Sniffer go in Logic?

The Audio Unit shows up as an instrument and as a MIDI processor. Put it in the MIDI FX slot of a drum track and it plays that track's instrument. Notes you send in pass straight through.

02 Formats

Which formats, and which Macs?

The installers that are up now are macOS 11 or newer, universal for Apple Silicon and Intel, signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. There is no Windows build in those installers.

  • Wild CardVST3 · CLAP · Audio Unit · Standalone · macOS
  • Conceal-N-StrikeVST3 · CLAP · Audio Unit · Standalone · macOS
  • BrunoVST3 · CLAP · Audio Unit (instrument and MIDI FX) · Standalone · Max for Live device in /Library/Application Support/Trilsoft/Bruno · macOS. The drum kit is built in.
  • MIDI Fart SnifferVST3 · CLAP · Audio Unit · Max for Live · Standalone · macOS

The Audio Unit is what Logic Pro and GarageBand load. Wild Card and Conceal-N-Strike also include a standalone app, the same instrument in its own window. MIDI Fart Sniffer's standalone app still sniffs without a host clock.

Can I leave a format out?

Yes. On the Installation Type step, hit Customize and skip the ones you don't want. Same move on all four.

04 Keys

How do I activate a plug-in?

Wild Card, Conceal-N-Strike, and Bruno put the activation code in the Buy Now email, and on the thank-you page right after checkout. First launch asks for it. Paste it and hit Activate. After that the license is checked offline. If the host swallows the paste shortcut, use the PASTE control.

MIDI Fart Sniffer asks for the code from the Get Free Key email. Paste it. After that the license stays on the machine.

The codes look like WILD-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX, CONC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX, BRUN-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX, and FART-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX.

What happens to my email address?

Checkout happens on Stripe's page. The license server uses the address once to send the key, then stores a keyed hash and a masked form like b•••@e•••.com. It does not keep the real address. Get Free Key works the same way: the address is used once to send the key, then only the hash and the mask are kept. The full write-up is on the privacy page.

I lost my key.

Email help@trilsoft.com with the plug-in name and the address you used for Buy Now or Get Free Key. That address can be matched to the license without the real one having been stored.
