Privacy

Tril$oft does not want your data. This website is not collecting it. We set no cookies of our own. There is no analytics, no advertising, no fingerprinting, and no tracking of who visits or what they do here.

Third-party services this site relies on have their own policies. Type is loaded from Google Fonts, and checkout is Stripe.

A purchase is handled by Stripe, on Stripe’s checkout page. Card numbers and other payment details go to Stripe. They do not come to Tril$oft. Read Stripe’s privacy policy.

The email address from that checkout is used for one thing: sending your license key. If you write in for help, we may use the same address to look the license up. We do not sell it, share it, or send marketing to it.

The plug-in contacts the license server only to activate a license. It does not track how you use the plug-in.

Questions, or a request to delete what we hold, go to help@trilsoft.com.
