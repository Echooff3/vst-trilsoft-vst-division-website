01 The carve

MAKE ROOM is a spectral sidechain ducker for iPad. It carves space in a mix the way a careful mix engineer would: only in the frequencies where the sidechain and the track actually collide.

Put it on a bass, a pad, a vocal, or a guitar. Feed it a kick, a lead, or any other source. Thirty-two bands listen across the spectrum. A band with energy in the sidechain ducks. A band that is clear stays up. The rest of the track keeps its weight.

02 The controls

  • AmountHow deep the carve goes.
  • Attack / ReleaseThey follow the sidechain.
  • Low / High fenceThe region you are willing to give up.
  • TiltLeans the duck toward the low end or the high end.
  • SensitivityHow quiet a sidechain has to be before a band reacts.
  • Mix / OutputThey sit after the carve.
  • BypassA delayed dry copy, so the host's delay compensation does not jump when you punch it in.

03 Latency

Latency is fixed and reported to the host. At 48 kHz it is 1,023 samples, about 21 milliseconds.

04 Where the sidechain comes from

Sidechain Source is the host's sidechain input when the host can wire one, including AUM, Logic Pro for iPad, and Cubasis. Some hosts cannot feed a second input to an Audio Unit.

MAKE ROOM Send is the companion effect for that case. Put Send on the source, MAKE ROOM on the destination, and pick the same slot, Send 1 through Send 8. The two plug-ins share audio on the iPad. Nothing leaves the device.

Both plug-ins are AUv3 effects, manufacturer Tril. The container app installs them. It does not play audio by itself.
