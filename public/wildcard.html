Step 01 · Setup

Run the installer

One macOS installer drops in every flavor: VST3, CLAP, an Audio Unit for Logic Pro and GarageBand, and a standalone app.

Only want some of them? Hit Customize on the Installation Type step and skip the rest.

Step 02 · Setup

Put it on a MIDI track

Wild Card is an instrument, so it goes where a synth would. In Ableton Live it's under Instruments ▸ Plug-ins. Open its window and you get the deck.

Or skip the DAW: the standalone app is the same deck in its own window.

Step 03 · Setup

Activate it once

The first time it opens, Wild Card asks for your activation code. It's in your purchase email, and it's on the thank-you page right after checkout.

Paste it in, hit Activate, and you're through. After that it checks your license offline.

  • PASTE for hosts that swallow ⌘V
  • WILD-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX
Step 04 · The record

Hit LOAD!

Pick a record: WAV, MP3, AIFF, FLAC, OGG or M4A, up to about 30 minutes long. The waveform fills the screen and the title lands on the caption.

Fresh instance? It opens the last file you loaded, so you're never staring at an empty deck.

Step 05 · Your controller

Plug in the Starlight

Connect your Hercules DJControl Starlight and watch the bubble: STARLIGHT HOOKED UP!

Wild Card talks to the controller directly, so there's no MIDI routing to set up in your DAW. It works the same in every host and in the standalone app.

Step 06 · Scratch

Touch it. Turn it.

Rest your hand on top of the jog wheel and you've got the record: the caption flips to GOT IT! and the readout says HOLDING. Let go and the motor takes it back.

  • TOUCH + TURN scratch
  • TURN THE EDGE nudge it faster or slower
  • SHIFT + TOUCH + TURN search 4× faster
Step 07 · No controller?

Scroll to scratch

Away from your gear? Scroll over the record with a mouse wheel or a two-finger trackpad swipe.

Scrolling grabs the record. Stop for a split second and it lets go, just like lifting your hand.

Step 08 · Transport

Play & cue

PLAY starts and stops the motor. CUE works like a CDJ: while it's playing, it jumps back to the cue and stops. Stopped somewhere else, it sets the cue right there. Sitting on the cue, hold it to preview.

  • SHIFT + PLAY play from the cue
  • SHIFT + CUE back to the start
Step 09 · Hot cues

Pads 1–4

Tap an empty pad to drop a hot cue where the needle is. Tap a lit one to jump there. Flags show up on the waveform so you can see where they are.

  • RIGHT-CLICK a pad to clear it
  • SHIFT + PAD clears it on the Starlight

They stick: your cues save with the project.

Step 10 · See it

Waveform & overview

The big view keeps the needle in the middle and slides the audio past it. The strip underneath is the whole track: click or drag it to move the playhead.

The readouts show live SPEED, PITCH, and whether the record is SPINNING, HOLDING or STOPPED.

Step 11 · Turntable

Tune the platter

  • START seconds for the motor to get up to speed
  • BRAKE seconds for the brake to stop it
  • SMOOTH how much the jog wheel is smoothed. Lower is tighter but grainier
  • SPEED how far the record moves per turn. 1.00× is a real 33⅓

Knobs: drag up or down, hold Shift for fine steps, double-click to reset.

Step 12 · Mixer

The scratch fader

The Starlight's crossfader is your cutting fader. Choose how it behaves:

  • FADER normal
  • HAMSTER reversed, for the other-way-round crowd
  • OFF ignore the crossfader
  • CURVE 0% is a smooth fade, 100% cuts in within the first 2% of travel

VOLUME sets the level. The bar shows the crossfader and your channel fader.

Step 13 · Vinyl mode

Grab it or bend it

VINYL MODE: ON and touching the wheel grabs the record. Flip it OFF and touching it only bends the speed, like nudging a CDJ.

  • VINYL button on the Starlight flips it too
Step 14 · Two decks

Deck A, Deck B

Load two Wild Cards. Set one to DECK A and the other to DECK B. Each follows its own jog wheel, pads and buttons on the one Starlight.

The crossfader then cuts between them, just like a real mixer.

Step 15 · Keep the take

Record your cuts

What comes out of Wild Card is the scratched audio. To keep a take, make an audio track that listens to the Wild Card track and hit record. Resampling or freeze-and-flatten work too.

Step 16 · Pro tips

A few extras

  • SPACE hold to cut the fader in the standalone app (in a DAW, Space belongs to the host)
  • PITCH the Starlight's pitch slider sets motor speed within the Pitch Range, ±8% out of the box
  • VOLUME the Starlight's channel fader is the channel volume
  • AUTOMATE the settings show up as DAW parameters
