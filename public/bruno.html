01 · SETUP

Run the installer

One macOS installer drops every format: VST3, CLAP, an Audio Unit, the standalone app, and the Max for Live device.

Skip what you don't play. Customize the Installation Type step and leave the rest out.

02 · SETUP

He plays your drums

Bruno doesn't make the sound. He plays MIDI into the drum plug-in you already like: EZdrummer, Superior Drummer, GetGood, Battery, a Drum Rack. Locked to the DAW's tempo and bars, in phrases, with fills.

No DAW? The standalone app plays him through its preview kit.

03 · SETUP

Activate once

The first launch asks for the code from your purchase email. Paste it in and the license stays on the machine.

  • BRUN-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX
04 · START

Count him in

The big button starts him. Press it again and he finishes on the next downbeat, with a crash. START WITH HOST starts him with the DAW's transport. Stopping the DAW always stops him.

Standalone app: Space starts and stops him.

05 · HIS MOOD

Calm to furious

The pad is his mood. Left to right is calm to furious, bottom to top is simple to fancy. Every dot is a groove from the section, and he plays the one nearest the puck. A move lands on the next bar.

Double-click the pad to put the puck back.

06 · SECTIONS

Call the section

Eight of them, INTRO to BREAKDOWN. Click one and it blinks until the next bar, then he's there. With FILLS up, he fills into it. PHRASE shows where he is in the phrase.

Standalone app: keys 1 to 8.

07 · HOW HARD

Fills, up to mania

FILLS at 0 is none. A little gives a short fill at the end of each phrase. Halfway, a full bar there and a short one halfway through. More, every other bar. All the way up, every bar is a fill: FILL MANIA.

FILL! asks for one right now. In the standalone app, so does F.

08 · VARIATION

Same take, or new

VARIATION at 0 plays the library as written. Up from there: looser timing and velocity, ghost notes, extra kicks, open hats, swapped crashes.

SAME TAKE plays the same performance every time the song plays, so a bounce matches what you heard. REROLL deals a different one. NEW TAKE rolls new dice every start.

09 · PHRASING

Bars and tempo

BARS: 4, 8, or 16 a phrase. START: wait for the next BAR or BEAT, or go NOW.

TEMPO: HOST follows the DAW. SET is the box: drag it. HALF and DOUBLE play it at half or double time.

10 · KEYSWITCHES

Boss him from the keys

  • C-1Start him, or stop him
  • C#-1A fill, now
  • D-1 – A-1Sections 1 to 8

FROM and CHANNEL move them. Last heard shows the last one, so a wrong octave is easy to spot. Every other note passes straight through.

11 · THE TAKE

Drag the take

Every bar he plays lands on the film strip, by the DAW's bar. Play a bar again and it's replaced. Grab DRAG ME and drop it on a track: a MIDI clip that starts at its first bar, with your output notes and channel.

CLEAR empties the strip.

12 · OUTPUT

Where the MIDI goes

CH is the channel he plays on (10, for drums). The percentage scales velocity.

The box under them copies the MIDI to a port: an IAC bus, a drum machine, or VIRTUAL PORT "Bruno", which other apps see as an input. The light flashes when MIDI goes out.

13 · MAP

Your drums, your notes

MAP sets the note each drum goes out on. It's General MIDI unless you change it, for drum plug-ins that put things elsewhere. Drag a note or click ‹ ›. GENERAL MIDI puts it all back.

14 · KIT

A kit to hear him

The preview kit is real drums: The Aasimonster, by DrumGizmo.org. It's OFF in a DAW, where your drum plug-in is the sound, and on in the standalone app. The slider is its volume.

15 · ABLETON

Right in front of the drums

Live won't put a third-party plug-in in the MIDI-effect slot, so the installer brings a Max for Live device. Drop it before your drums. OPEN BRUNO shows the window. PLAY, STOP and FILL! work without it.

No Max for Live? Bruno on one track, drums on another: set the drums' MIDI From to Bruno's track, pick Bruno under it, monitor In.

16 · LOGIC

Use the MIDI FX slot

The Audio Unit shows up as an instrument and as a MIDI processor. Put it in the MIDI FX slot of a drum track and he plays that track's instrument.

Keyswitches from the region. Put C-1 and friends in the MIDI region and he follows the song.
